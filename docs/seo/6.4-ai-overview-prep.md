# AI Overview & Answer Engine Optimization Prep

AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE) do not click links; they extract semantic chunks. We must format our programmatic pages for extraction.

## The AI Citation Target
To be cited by an AI, a page needs:
1. High domain authority (handled by our cluster strategy).
2. Machine-readable data structures (JSON-LD).
3. "Answer-first" formatting (No fluff before the data).

## Answer-First Formatting Strategy

### Before (Traditional SEO):
*H2: How much is the EMI for 50 Lakhs?*
*"Taking a loan is a big decision. Many people wonder what the EMI is. Based on a tenure of 10 years and an interest rate of 8.5%, the EMI for 50 Lakhs is ₹61,993."*

### After (AEO/GEO Optimized):
*H2: What is the EMI for a 50 Lakh Home Loan for 10 Years?*
*"**The monthly EMI for a ₹50 Lakh home loan for 10 years at an 8.5% interest rate is ₹61,993.** The total interest payable over the tenure is ₹24,39,124, making the total repayment amount ₹74,39,124."*

## Structural Changes for Tool Pages
1. **Remove Intro Fluff:** The tool MUST be the very first element in the DOM tree inside the `<main>` tag.
2. **Data Tables:** Ensure the Amortization schedule is rendered as a clean HTML `<table>`, not complex nested `<div>` grids, as LLMs parse standard HTML tables perfectly.
