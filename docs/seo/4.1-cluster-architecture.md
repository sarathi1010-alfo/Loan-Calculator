# Cluster Architecture: Loan Tools

This maps out the internal linking graph and semantic structure for our Loan Calculator topical monopoly.

## Hub Page
**URL:** `/home-loan-calculator`
**Title:** The Ultimate Home Loan EMI Calculator & Guide
**Content Type:** Deep, manually written guide + Interactive master calculator.
**Links Out To:** Top 20 most popular Spoke Tools.

## Level 1 Spokes (Bank Modifiers)
**URL Example:** `/home-loan-calculator/sbi-home-loan-emi-calculator`
**Content Type:** SSR/ISR interactive calculator with bank-specific default interest rates.
**Links Up To:** Hub
**Links Across To:** `hdfc-home-loan-emi-calculator`, `icici-home-loan-emi-calculator`
**Links Down To:** Specific amount/tenure combinations.

## Level 2 Spokes (Amount + Tenure Modifiers - High Volume Programmatic)
**URL Example:** `/home-loan-calculator/50-lakh-sbi-home-loan-emi-for-10-years`
**Content Type:** Programmatically generated page from `seo-pages.json`. Calculator is pre-filled, pie chart is pre-rendered.
**Links Up To:** Level 1 Spoke (`sbi-home-loan-emi-calculator`)
**Links Across To:** `50-lakh-hdfc-home-loan-emi-for-10-years` (Same Amount, Diff Bank), `40-lakh-sbi-home-loan-emi-for-10-years` (Diff Amount, Same Bank).

## Supporting Information Nodes (Guides)
To build Topical Authority, we must have informational content, not just tools.
- `/home-loan-calculator/guide/how-to-reduce-home-loan-emi`
- `/home-loan-calculator/guide/floating-vs-fixed-interest-rates`
- `/home-loan-calculator/guide/cibil-score-impact-on-home-loan`

*These guides link directly to the Level 1 spoke calculators to drive authority.*
